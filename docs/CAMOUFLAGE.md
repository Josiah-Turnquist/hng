# Camouflage: what is real and what is not

The game has fifteen camouflage patterns: three that soldiers wear by default
and twelve more a uniform can be made in (`src/actors/soldierCamo.js`). This
page says how close each is to the real thing, so nobody takes the game for a
reference.

## How the patterns were made

They are drawn in code from memory of what the real patterns look like: the
kind of shapes, how many colors, roughly which colors. None was checked
against a photograph or a surviving garment. They are recognizable in kind.
They are not copies.

## Who really wore each one

| Pattern in the game | Real pattern | Who wore it | On what |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Splinter (default) | Splittertarn, 1931 | German army and air force | Shelter sheets, smocks, helmet covers, paratrooper jump smocks |
| Marsh (default) | Sumpftarn, 1943 | German army | Smocks, helmet covers, winter suits |
| Oak leaf, spring and autumn | Eichenlaubmuster | Waffen-SS only | Smocks, helmet covers, shelter sheets; reversible, one side for each season |
| Plane tree, spring and autumn | Platanenmuster | Waffen-SS only | Smocks, helmet covers, shelter sheets; reversible |
| Pea dot | Erbsenmuster, 1944 | Waffen-SS only | A full uniform: tunic and trousers |
| Italian | Telo mimetico, 1929 | Italian army; German troops from late 1943, on captured cloth | Shelter sheets, and uniforms tailored from them |
| Frog skin, green and brown | US M1942 | Mostly US Marines in the Pacific. In Europe only a few units, briefly, in Normandy in summer 1944 | A full uniform; reversible |
| Painted jump suit | Not a printed pattern | Some US paratroopers, who daubed their own suits with paint | The jump suit |
| Amoeba (default) and amoeba, autumn | MKK, 1938 | Soviet scouts, snipers, combat engineers, airborne | A loose suit worn over the uniform |
| Leaf | Soviet leaf pattern, 1940 to 1941 | The same specialists | The same kind of oversuit |
| Three-color, 1944 | TTsMKK, 1944 | The same specialists, late in the war | The same kind of oversuit |

## Where the game departs from history

- **Whole uniforms.** In the game a pattern covers tunic, sleeves, trousers,
  pockets and helmet. In reality most camouflage was a smock, a helmet cover
  or an oversuit worn over the plain uniform. The pea dot uniform, the frog
  skin uniform and uniforms tailored from Italian cloth are the exceptions.
- **Any soldier can wear any of his army's patterns.** In reality oak leaf,
  plane tree and pea dot were Waffen-SS patterns, not worn by the regular
  army whose insignia the game's German soldiers carry. Soviet patterns were
  for scouts, snipers and engineers, not ordinary riflemen. Painted suits
  were paratroopers' only.
- **Frog skin in Europe was rare.** It was tried by parts of the US 2nd
  Armored Division in Normandy and withdrawn within months. The usual
  explanation is that it looked too much like German camouflage; that is
  widely repeated but not firmly documented.
- **The painted jump suit is a guess at a look, not a pattern.** Surviving
  suits and accounts show that some were painted. There was no standard.
