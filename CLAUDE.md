# Rank & File: rules for working on this project

## Design rules

- **No colored one-sided borders.** Never put a colored line on just one side
  of a box, row, tab, card or field: no `border-left`, `border-top` and so on
  in a color, no one-sided `inset` box-shadow stripe, no thin colored bar
  pinned to one edge. To mark something (selected, in battle, active tab, a
  win or loss), use a full border all the way round, a background tint, or the
  text color. Plain grey divider lines between rows are fine.
- Gold buttons all look like the "Ready for battle" button: the gold gradient
  with black text.
- Don't add options for the player to customize unless asked.

## Writing

- Plain English, no industry jargon, in messages, the game and the docs.
- Never use em dashes in anything written for the project.

## Working

- Send screenshots whenever something visible changes.
- Don't change the game's numbers just to change them; they get tuned in
  testing.
